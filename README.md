# Sprout - App Background & Overview

## What is Sprout?

Sprout is a mobile gardening app that helps users manage their personal gardens with elegance and delight. At its core, Sprout makes it easy to track plants, set watering reminders, and document growth over time through photos - all wrapped in a smooth, premium user experience with a charming leafling mascot.

---

## Purpose & Problem Solving

**Primary Problems Addressed:**

- Forgetting to water plants and maintain regular care schedules
- Difficulty tracking plant growth and progress over time
- Lack of organized system for managing multiple plants
- Wanting a delightful, elegant app experience (vs cluttered or overly complex garden apps)

**Core Value Proposition:**
Sprout takes the complexity out of plant care by providing simple reminders, visual progress tracking, and an enjoyable user experience that makes gardening feel rewarding rather than overwhelming.

---

## Target Audience

**Primary Users:**

- All experience levels (beginners to experienced gardeners)
- All garden types (indoor, outdoor, balcony, greenhouse, etc.)
- Users who appreciate premium, thoughtfully-designed apps
- People who want to be more intentional and organized with their plant care

**User Profile:**

- Age range: All ages (designed to be accessible and appealing across demographics)
- Tech comfort: Comfortable with mobile apps
- Gardening motivation: From casual plant owners to dedicated gardeners
- Values: Organization, simplicity, visual progress, delightful experiences

---

## What Makes Sprout Different

**Key Differentiators:**

1. **Smooth & Elegant UX** - Premium feel with thoughtful animations, gestures, and haptics
2. **Leafling Mascot** - A charming character that adds personality to loading states, empty states, and user interactions
3. **Simplicity First** - Focused on core features done exceptionally well, avoiding feature bloat
4. **Phased Approach** - Starting personal, with vision for social/community features later

**vs Other Garden Apps:**

- Planta/Vera: More complex, database-heavy, can feel overwhelming
- Sprout: Clean, simple, focused on your personal garden experience with room to grow

---

## Development Goals

**Primary Objectives:**

- Build full-stack mobile development skills (Expo + backend)
- Create a portfolio piece showcasing premium UX/UI design
- Learn advanced React Native techniques (Reanimated, Gesture Handler, etc.)
- Ship a real product that solves a genuine problem

**Secondary Objectives:**

- Potentially gain real users and validate product-market fit
- Open to scaling if the app gains traction
- Generate sustainable income to cover costs (and possibly beyond)

**Philosophy:**
This is a side project built for learning and enjoyment first, with the flexibility to become something bigger if users find real value in it.

---

## Platform Strategy

**Phase 1:** iOS only

- Focus on single platform to move faster
- iOS-first allows for polished experience before expanding
- Expo makes future Android port straightforward

**Phase 2:** Android

- Expand to Android once iOS version is stable and validated
- Leverage Expo's cross-platform capabilities

---

## Product Phases

### Phase 1: Personal Garden Manager (MVP)

**Scope:**

- Add and manage plants (up to 10-15 in free tier)
- Set watering reminders with notifications
- Upload photos and track progress over time
- Simple, elegant UI with leafling mascot throughout
- Core functionality completely free

**Goal:** Nail the personal garden management experience. Make it feel premium, delightful, and genuinely useful.

### Phase 2: Social & Community (Future)

**Scope:**

- Share gardens with friends
- Visit other people's gardens
- Follow/discover other gardeners
- Community features and interactions

**Goal:** Transform Sprout from personal tool to social gardening platform.

---

## Success Criteria

**Phase 1:**

- App feels smooth and premium (60fps animations, great haptics)
- Users (starting with girlfriend + early testers) find it genuinely useful
- Core features work reliably (reminders actually remind, photos save properly)
- Leafling mascot adds charm without feeling gimmicky
- Strong foundation for future social features

**Long-term:**

- Growing user base who actively use the app
- Positive App Store reviews highlighting UX quality
- Sustainable freemium model (covers costs, potential income)
- Portfolio piece that demonstrates full-stack mobile skills

---

## Technical Approach

**Frontend:**

- Expo / React Native
- Reanimated 3 for smooth animations
- Gesture Handler for intuitive interactions
- Expo Haptics for tactile feedback

**Backend:**

- Supabase
- Focus on speed of development while maintaining quality
- Scalable architecture for future social features

**Design:**

- Smooth and elegant aesthetic
- Minimal, intentional use of animations
- Leafling mascot integrated throughout
- Premium feel with playful touches

---

## Origin Story

Sprout was born from a real need: my girlfriend recently got into gardening and wanted a way to track her plants, set reminders, and share progress with friends. As a frontend developer looking to build full-stack mobile skills, this became the perfect opportunity to create something useful while learning - a project that solves a genuine problem while being genuinely enjoyable to build.
